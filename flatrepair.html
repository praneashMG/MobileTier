REPAIR OR REPLACE?

Know When to Fix vs Replace

Not all flat tires can be safely repaired. Our certified techs follow industry safety standards to help you make the right decision.

Can repair:

Puncture within tread area, less than 1/4" diameter, single puncture per tire

Must replace:

Sidewall damage, large gashes, multiple punctures, run-flat damage

Our Promise: We'll never recommend unnecessary replacement. Free inspection before any work.
